This Code of Conduct explains the expectations for participation in the apm community and ecosystem operated by apm.to Inc. (apm).

## Our Pledge

We want apm participation to be respectful, constructive, safe, and welcoming.

This applies across public and private interactions connected to apm, including:

- documentation and support channels
- package publishing and maintainership
- issue reporting and collaboration spaces
- events, chat, and email interactions

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- focusing on what is best for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, or threats
- trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- hate speech or discriminatory conduct
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- publishing others' private information without permission
- abusive or disruptive conduct that undermines a professional environment

## Enforcement Responsibilities

apm may investigate reported conduct and take fair action when needed to protect the community and the service.

Actions may include:

- warnings
- content removal
- account or package restrictions
- temporary suspension
- permanent removal from specific spaces or services

## Reporting

Please report concerns to [support@abappm.com](mailto:support@abappm.com).

Include as much context as you can, such as:

- what happened
- where it happened
- when it happened
- who was involved
- links, screenshots, or other supporting material if available

You are not expected to confront abusive behavior on your own.

## Scope

This policy applies to community participation related to apm, including public content, private support interactions, package maintainership, and contributor collaboration.

## Relationship to Other Policies

This Code of Conduct works alongside the:

- [Terms of Use](/policies/terms-of-use.html)
- [Package Name Disputes](/policies/package-name-disputes.html)
- [Security](/policies/security.html)

apm reserves the right to interpret and apply these policies in good faith to protect users and the broader ecosystem.

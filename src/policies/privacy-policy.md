This Privacy Policy describes how apm.to, Inc. (apm) collects, uses, and protects personal information.

## Information We May Collect

Depending on the service, apm may collect:

- account information such as name, email address, and profile data
- billing and subscription information
- package, registry, and account activity
- support communications
- technical and security information such as logs, IP addresses, browser details, and diagnostic events

## How We Use Information

apm may use information to:

- provide and operate the service
- authenticate users and secure accounts
- process billing and subscription management
- improve reliability, safety, and support
- investigate abuse, fraud, or policy violations
- comply with legal obligations

## Sharing

apm does **not** sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers, payment processors, infrastructure providers, or legal authorities when needed to operate the service or comply with law.

## Retention

apm retains information for as long as reasonably needed for service operation, security, billing, dispute handling, and legal compliance.

## Security

apm takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

See [Security](/policies/security.html) for related security expectations.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to [support@abappm.com](mailto:support@abappm.com).

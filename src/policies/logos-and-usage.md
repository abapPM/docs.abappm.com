This policy explains how names, logos, and brand assets associated with apm.to Inc. (apm) may be used.

## Allowed Uses

You may refer to apm in factual ways, such as:

- describing compatibility
- linking to apm services or documentation
- discussing packages, tools, or integrations that work with apm

## Not Allowed Without Permission

You may not, without prior written permission:

- imply partnership, endorsement, or certification by apm
- use apm logos in a way that suggests official status
- use confusingly similar branding for packages, organizations, websites, or services
- use apm branding in a misleading, defamatory, or abusive context

## Trademark Protection

apm reserves all rights in its names, logos, and brand identifiers except where explicit permission is granted.

Questions about brand use can be sent to [support@abappm.com](mailto:support@abappm.com?subject=Branding).

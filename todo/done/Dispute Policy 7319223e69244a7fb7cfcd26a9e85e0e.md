# Dispute Resolution

This document describes the steps that you should take to resolve naming disputes with other apm publishers. It also describes the steps you should take if you think a name [infringes your trademark](https://abappm.com/disputes).

This document is additive to the guidelines in the [Code of Conduct](https://abappm.com/conduct) and [Terms of Use](https://abappm.com/terms). Nothing in this document should be interpreted to contradict any aspect of the Code of Conduct or Terms of Use.

# TLDR

1. Send an email to support@abappm.com
2. Provide as much detail as possible (see [name disputes](https://support.github.com/contact/npm-name-disputes) for an example of what information is useful)
3. Support will address your request. Please note submitting a report does not guarantee the transfer of a package, organization, or username.

# **When to use this process**

This process is an excellent way to:

- Request a name that you believe is currently misleading or could be confused with a name used by your company or open source project
- Request a name related to your company or open source project that cannot be claimed via account recovery

This process does not apply if the package violates our [Terms of Use](https://abappm.com/terms), in particular our Acceptable Use and Acceptable Content rules, or our [Code of Conduct](https://abappm.com/conduct). Those documents refer to this one to resolve cases of "squatting"; see below.

If you see bad behavior or content you believe is unacceptable, refer to the Code of Conduct for guidelines on reporting violations. **You are never expected to resolve abusive behavior on your own.** **We are here to help.**

# **When not to use this process**

This process is not available for dispute requests due to lack of activity related to a specific name.

Please also note there are cases where a party may have claim to a specific name, but giving that name to the requesting party would pose a supply-chain risk to the apm ecosystem. In such cases, requests may be denied independent of the validity of the claim.

# **Trademarks**

apm processes trademark claims under our [Trademark Policy](https://abappm.com/trademarks).

If you think another apm publisher is infringing your trademark, such as by using a confusingly similar package, organization, or user account name, please submit a Trademark Policy Violation Report via email to [support@abappm.com](mailto:support@abappm.com?subject=Trademark) [trademark policy violation](https://support.github.com/contact/trademark-policy) for an example of what information is useful).

Use of apm's own trademarks is covered by our [Logo and Usage Policy](https://docs.npmjs.com/policies/logos-and-usage).

# **Definitions**

### **Squatting**

It is against apm's [Terms of Use](https://abappm.com/terms) to publish a package, register a user name or an organization name simply for the purposes of reserving it for future use.

We do not pro-actively scan the registry for squatted packages, so the fact that a name is in use does not mean we consider it valid. The standards for what we consider squatting depend on what is being squatted:

### **Packages**

Package names are considered squatted if the package has no genuine function.

### **Organizations**

Organization names are considered squatted if there are no packages published within a reasonable time. If an organization is a paid organization, it may have private packages that are invisible to third parties. For privacy reasons, we cannot reveal whether or not an organization has private packages, so a paid organization will never be considered squatted.

### **User names**

We are extremely unlikely to transfer control of a user name, as it is totally valid to be an apm user and never publish any packages: for instance, you might be part of an organization or need read-only access to private packages.

Replication and Crawler Policy

This policy describes how apm.to Inc. (apm) expects automated access, crawling, mirroring, and large-scale replication of apm content to be handled.

Allowed Behavior

Reasonable automated access for indexing, caching, monitoring, or integration may be allowed when it:

  • respects technical controls and rate limits
  • does not degrade service performance
  • does not bypass authentication or access restrictions
  • does not copy or republish protected content in misleading ways

Not Allowed

You may not:

  • scrape or mirror apm at a scale that harms service availability
  • bypass robots, access controls, or subscription rules
  • republish registry, account, or policy content in ways that misrepresent its source
  • use automation to probe security weaknesses or harvest user data

Enforcement

apm may throttle, block, or otherwise restrict automated access that creates risk, abuse, or operational strain.

Contact

Questions about crawler, mirroring, or replication use cases can be sent to support@abappm.com.
